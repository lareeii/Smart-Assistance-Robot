    SOMA

    Smart Butler: The discreet assistant that looks after you

    Discover the future of personal care with our Smart Assistance Doctor Robot. SOMA combines advanced robotic technology with an intelligent wearable device to provide continuous, non-invasive monitoring of your vital signs. With SOMA, you'll have a health assistant always by your side: detecting important changes, guiding you through basic medical tests, and alerting you to any situation that requires attention, all from the comfort of your home.

    Designed to fit seamlessly into your daily life, SOMA not only cares for your health but does so in an intuitive, reliable, and personalized way. Make your well-being a priority with the peace of mind that only an intelligent assistant can provide.

    Idle Mode

    SOMA remains in standby, continuously monitoring your baseline health data in the background. Always ready to respond when needed.

    Welcome Interface

    A friendly and intuitive entry point. SOMA greets the user, provides a quick status overview, and guides the next interaction.

    Active Monitoring

    SOMA is fully engaged, guiding the user through measurements, analyzing data in real time, and providing immediate feedback and recommendations.

    Monitoring Methods

    • Wristband → continuous & preventive measurement
    • Robot / accessories → precise guided measurements
    • Non-invasive & scalable for all users
    • Redundancy: wristband + robot complement each other

    Smartwatch

    • Heart rate & variability (stress, fatigue, recovery)
    • Oxygen saturation (SpO₂)
    • Body temperature trends
    • Activity tracking & sleep quality
    • Fall detection & location for emergencies

    Smart Butler Robot

    • Blood pressure & basic ECG
    • Blood glucose measurement guided by robot
    • Respiratory rate & patterns monitoring
    • Precise body temperature

    Guided Tests

    • Visual explanation for sensor placement
    • Adjust position, height & duration
    • Validation of measurement & error repeat
    • Call medical assistance if necessary
